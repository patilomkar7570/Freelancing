## About The Project

AUREA Orthodontics is a completely original, high-craft digital brand identity and healthcare website concept designed for a specialist orthodontic practice.

### Key Highlights
- **Original Initials-Based Monogram (AO):** Combines an architectural parabolic dental arch (`A`) with a continuous harmonic circle (`O`) and a golden smile arc crossbar.
- **Curated Healthcare Palette:** Built around Warm Ivory (`#F8F6F2`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Blush (`#F2E8E9`), Charcoal (`#252326`), and selective Primary Burgundy (`#702D3B`) / Deep Burgundy (`#421D29`).
- **Typography:** Instrument Serif (editorial display), Manrope (architectural headings), and Inter (clinical body text).
- **Interactive Features:**
  - 3D Discovery Consultation Booking Drawer
  - Smile Pathway Navigator Quiz
  - Anatomical Bite Visualizer
  - Braces Food Safety Checker & Search
  - Orthodontic Emergency Triage & Relief Guide
  - Searchable Categorized FAQ Accordion
  - Simulated Smile Gallery & Treatment Case Modals
- **Hosting:** Hosted live globally on **Cloudflare Pages**.
