## 🌲 Project Overview

**Kuura Riverside Resort** is an original hospitality design concept situated along the bank of the Kemijoki River amidst the boreal forests of Finnish Lapland. Designed to showcase modern Scandinavian aesthetic principles, the concept features autonomous timber duplex cottages, panoramic lodge suites, riverside camping grounds, authentic wood-heated saunas, and year-round Arctic recreation.

* **Scandinavian Editorial Aesthetics:** Warm ivory (`#F5F2E9`), deep spruce forest green (`#263D32`), pine green (`#405B48`), subtle cabin red (`#A4513B`), and river mist blue (`#9EB8B5`).
* **Original Minimalist Visual Identity:** The *Pohjantähti* (Northern Star) shining above a minimalist timber fell gable, grounded by the flowing ribbon currents of the Kemijoki River.
* **Client-Side Interactive Journey:** Non-operational reservation enquiry interface with validation, date logic, and clear demonstration disclosures.
