## Featured Projects

### 1. AUREA Orthodontics
**Live Website:** [https://aurea-orthodontics.pages.dev](https://aurea-orthodontics.pages.dev)  
**Project Folder:** [`/aurea-orthodontics`](./aurea-orthodontics)  
**Industry:** Healthcare / Specialist Orthodontic Practice  
**Design & Stack:** Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Cloudflare Pages

#### Overview
A completely original, high-craft brand identity and comprehensive multi-page website concept for a premium orthodontic clinic. Featuring an original geometric **AO** monogram, interactive clinical tools, and medically conservative patient education.

#### Key Capabilities & Features
- **Original Brand System:** Custom initials-based monogram (*parabolic arch + harmonic circle ligature*), 7 logo variations, and curated healthcare palette (Deep Burgundy `#702D3B`, Warm Ivory `#F8F6F2`, Soft Blush `#F2E8E9`, Charcoal `#252326`).
- **Interactive 3D Consultation Booking:** Multi-step triage flow for patient categories, concerns, and appointment windows.
- **Interactive Bite Visualizer:** Anatomical vector illustrations for 7 malocclusion conditions (crowding, deep overbite, underbite, crossbite, open bite, overjet, spacing).
- **Interactive Food Safety Checker:** Searchable database filtering safe foods, items to prepare with care, and high-risk foods to avoid.
- **Interactive Emergency Triage:** Step-by-step home relief instructions, illustrated relief wax tutorial, and clinical urgency levels.
- **Searchable Categorized FAQ:** Real-time filter with accessible animated accordions.
- **Demonstration Smile Gallery:** Simulated aesthetic case studies with clinical objectives.
- **Patient Resource Downloads:** Welcome guide, checklist, and appointment prep outlines.
